Quick start

Create a Better Result

Three simple steps to get started, plus a practical guide for choosing the alpha value.

01 · Choose your content

Upload the photo you want to transform. Start with a sharp, well-lit image where the main subject is easy to see. For portraits, keep the face unobstructed.

02 · Choose a style reference

Select a painting, illustration, or textured image whose colours and brushwork you want to borrow. The style image guides the artistic appearance.

03 · Set strength and generate

Move the Style Strength slider, then press Transfer Style. Wait for processing to finish, review the result, and use Download Result.

Alpha guide · Choose a starting point
0.00 Original content photo. No style transfer is applied.
0.15–0.35 Subtle starting range when you want to retain more of the original appearance.
0.40–0.65 A balanced range to explore content and style together.
0.70–1.00 Strong stylization. Fine details and facial features may change more.
Helpful tip: Every content/style pair behaves differently. For portraits, try a lower alpha first and increase it gradually. The live result may differ depending on the current model and alpha.
